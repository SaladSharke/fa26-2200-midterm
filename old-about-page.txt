            <h3>BTU RULES & POLICIES</h3>

            <h4>RULES</h4>
            <ol>
                <li>Respect others, their space, and their work.</li>
                <li>Observe <b>safety</b> for you, others, and equipment.</li>
                <li>PLEASE...report problems with <b>equipment</b>.</li>
                <li>Remember, using slang speech OR pop culture as an excuse for denigrating people identifying with a race, age group, gender, sexual orientation, physical ability, etc., is not for the BTU.</li>
                <li>Report taking the last "____________". ex. Glue stick.</li>
                <li>First ask approval to remove any tool or material from the lab.</li>
                <li>Protect surfaces from hot glue, paint, marmots, etc.</li>
                <li><b>Keep common work areas clear of your stuff, when you can.</b></li>
                <li><b>Put things back where they belong, not just where you found them.</b></li>
                <li>Let the staff know if anyone is not being cool.</li>
                <li>Clear scraps of material on a common work area.</li>
                <li>Sweep up scraps of material on the floor.</li>
                <li>Keep it clean.</li>
                <li>Use tools, materials and processes as intended, when appropriate (a.k.a get trained!).</li>
                <li>Did we mention, "get trained"? That's why you're here!</li>
                <li>Leave out anything else, not listed here, that negatively affects the BTU, ATLAS, CU, and EARTH community. Please, do not make us make a rule.</li>
            </ol>

            <h4>POLICIES</h4>
            <ol>
                <li>BTU Lab observes all <a href="https://www.cu.edu/ope/aps/2027">policies and codes of conduct of Colorado University</a>.</li>
                <li>Access to BTU will be removed for anyone repeatedly disregarding lab rules and policies and/or disrespecting others.</li>
            </ol>
        </section>

            <h2>Faculty</h2>

            <h3>Welcome, and welcome back</h3>

            <p>
                The BTU Lab exists to facilitate as much student, faculty and staff coursework, research and experimentation as possible. 
                <b>We support as many as 7000 coursework hours</b> per semester.
            </p>
            <p>
                Because of this incredible demand, expectations for students, including yours, and faculty are covered in a series of <b>brief, mandatory, one-time orientations.</b>
            </p>
            <p>
                In addition, for faculty, this page is set up to help you identify needs for yourself and your students and to direct you to the necessary next steps for the our collective success.
            </p>
            <p>
                As always, if you have any questions please contact me at "<a href="mailto:zack.weaver@colorado.edu">zack.weaver@colorado.edu</a>". Welcome, and welcome back, to the ATLAS Institute and the BTU Lab.
            </p>
            <p>Zack Weaver,</p>

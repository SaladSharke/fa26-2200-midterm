Services & Workshops

Below is a list of the utilities and services provided by the BTU.




TOOLS

General Tools equipment and materials are labeled GREEN.

We can accommodate dozens of people at once and are working on increasing project storage space.




WOOD SHOP

The Wood Shop entrance is marked in PINK. Inside, tools have their own color-coding system.

Access to certain machines is facilitated by faculty, anyone can become a "Mischief Maker" on any machine.

WOOD SHOP ACCESS is separate from general BTU access and REQUIRES AN ORIENTATION.

Major equipment is shut off after the hours of 10pm and 6am daily.



Wood Shop Equipment

  • Table Saw
  • Band Saw
  • Drill Press
  • Chop Saw
  • Belt Sander
  • Random Orbit Sander
  • Palm Router
  • Drills/Drivers
  • Hand Saws
  • Files
  • Measuring Tools
  • Other hand tools



3D PRINTING

Printing equipment and materials are labeled in PURPLE.

BTU uses PLA and ABS material, other materials are to be agreed upon with BTU faculty.

Do not alter any part of the machine, Do not train yourself on the machine, Do not use third-party slicing software.



3D PRINTING EQUIPMENT AND MATERIALS

  • Prusa Mk4 FDM printer (x3)
  • Prusa Mk3S+ FDM printer (x3)
  • Prusa MINI2 FDM printer (x4)
  • Additional FDM printers available for experimental projects. See staff!
  • 1.75mm PLA filament (black, and random colors)
  • 1.75mm ABS filament (black, and random colors)
  • Additional materials allowed only with staff approval.



ELECTRONICS

Electronics and coding training happens as requested by staff and/or community members. It is also the subject of SOME ATLAS CLASSES and Workshops.
